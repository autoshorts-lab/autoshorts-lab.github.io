Fido-X Publisher

Fido-X Publisher is a small private automation tool used by the FunForAll channels to publish short, family-friendly videos hosted by Fido-X, an AI-assisted dog character.

The tool uploads videos only to the channel owner's own YouTube and Instagram accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's data.
