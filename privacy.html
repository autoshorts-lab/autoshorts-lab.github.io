Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Fido-X Publisher ("the app") is a private publishing tool operated by the owner of the FunForAll channels. It is used only with the operator's own accounts.

What data the app uses

  • YouTube (Google): the app uses Google OAuth access to upload videos to the operator's own YouTube channel and to read that channel's own video statistics (views, watch time) to improve future videos.
  • Instagram (Meta): the app uses access to the operator's own Instagram professional account to publish Reels.

The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of viewers or any other users. Access tokens are stored encrypted and used only to perform the actions above.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions or in Instagram's settings under apps and websites.
